## Method, stated plainly

- Measurements come from **our own probes in a real, logged-out browser** — the answer a cold-start
  buyer actually sees. Vendor APIs sample a different surface; we do not treat the two as
  interchangeable, and we say which one a number came from.
- Every published number carries its **date, sample size, and the query set**. Small samples are
  labelled as small samples.
- **We do not sell rankings.** No one can guarantee a position inside a generative answer, and we
  do not claim otherwise.
- When we get something wrong, the correction stays on the page with a date. Silently deleting a
  number you no longer like is the actual credibility problem.
